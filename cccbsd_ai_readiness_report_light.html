What "AI Readiness" actually means

AI Readiness isn't about whether you use ChatGPT. It's about whether the work you actually do is set up to benefit from AI – so it saves your team time, raises the quality of what you produce, and gives back hours for the mission and people work that matters most.

Five conditions matter:

  • Your data is usable. AI can't work with information that lives only in someone's head.
  • You know where your time is going. Repetitive tasks you can name are tasks you can delegate.
  • You know your community. Your families, donors, and referral partners – who they are, what they need, and how they find you.
  • There's a clear way you do things. AI amplifies what's repeatable. If your methods live in one person's head, there's nothing to amplify yet.
  • You have momentum. You've tried things, you're curious, and you want to move.

What the rest of the diagnostic confirms

The hard-systems conditions are strong – you're already using AI, your data and CRM are clean, SOPs exist, and your team reviews the numbers regularly. The one place time is going is content production: "templates, but they still take a lot of time." For a Director of Advancement, that's appeals, the annual report, grant proposals and reporting, newsletters, event promotion, and the 150-year-anniversary campaign now underway. None of the opportunity areas this points to require putting student records, IEP or clinical data, or donor PII into an AI tool – that's a hard boundary, and the plan is built to respect it.

Where the leverage is

The opportunity areas

Four areas where the diagnostic surfaced real leverage – each tagged with its source. These are directional, not finished specs; the AI Plan is where they get prioritized and scoped into actual builds.

1

Advancement & Communications Engine

Anchor opportunity · Speaks directly to the magic wand and the biggest time drain
AI Readiness Check

The direct answer to "communications overhaul" and the "templates, but they still take a lot of time" answer. An AI-assisted drafting layer for the work the advancement office produces every week: appeals, the annual report, newsletters, donor and grant acknowledgments, event promotion, and social content – all in CCCBSD's established voice, all routed for human approval. It turns your template library into a guided workflow that drafts the first 80% and leaves the judgment, personalization, and sign-off with your team. Tuned first to the 150-year-anniversary campaign you're running right now. This is the area that gives the most time back.

2

Grant & Development Writing System

Foundation opportunity · Speaks to the sustainability priority in your strategic plan
AI Readiness Check Public research

You were just named a Cummings Foundation grant recipient, and foundation, corporate, and community sponsors are a core funding channel; sustainability is a named strategic-plan priority. A structured system for grant research, LOI and proposal drafting, narratives pulled from a maintained library of program facts and outcomes, and – the part that quietly eats development time – grant reporting after the award. AI drafts; your team verifies every claim against source. This area protects the funding engine the mission depends on.

3

Reputation & Visibility System

Time-leverage opportunity · The clearest fix on your weakest condition
AI Readiness Check Public research

Knowing your community was your one condition below "ready to scale," held down entirely by the absence of structured online reviews. Public research shows the reputation is already there – 150 years, a 98%-recommend Facebook page, a WCVB Chronicle feature. This area captures it: a guided way to invite Google reviews from families and alumni, a maintained presence on GuideStar / Candid, Charity Navigator, and Great Nonprofits where funders check, AI-drafted responses in your voice, and a repeatable way to turn alumni and milestone stories into structured testimonials. The reputation is built; this puts it to work.

4

Family & Community Communications + Accessibility

Acceleration opportunity · Built for how a Deaf-and-disability-serving school communicates
AI Readiness Check

You serve families across 40 communities and value ASL and English equally; your site already runs Google Translate. An AI-assisted workflow for family-facing communications – program updates, event notices, family-resource materials, and PIP and community-program outreach – produced faster and made more accessible: plain-language versions, multilingual drafts for the languages your families actually speak, and a documented standard for what AI may and may not touch when communicating about students. Closes the production gap on your highest-trust, most sensitive communications. Operates on program and event content – never student records, IEP, or clinical data.
